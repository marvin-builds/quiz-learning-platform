# Seed image sources

The photos in `supabase/seed-images/` that the showcase quizzes in
`supabase/seed.sql` use. All are in the public domain as works of U.S.
federal agencies, taken from Wikimedia Commons and resized to at most 800 px
with their metadata stripped. This file sits outside the folder because
`supabase start` uploads everything in it to the `quiz-images` bucket.
